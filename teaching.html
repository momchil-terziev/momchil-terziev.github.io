Hydrodynamics tutorials

1. Dispersion relation

Derivation of ω² = gk tanh(kh) from Laplace's equation with the bed, kinematic and dynamic boundary conditions, followed by the velocity components and pressure under the wave.

2. Wavelength of surface waves for a translating body

The wavelength of the waves that stay stationary relative to a moving body, λ = 2πu²cos²θ / g.

3. Kelvin wave angle

Group velocity from two superposed waves, and the Kelvin half-angle of 19°28′ from the ray angle of the wave packets.

4. Limiting hull speed

The speed at which the transverse wavelength equals the ship length, umax = 1.25√L.

5. Humps and hollows

Interference between the bow and stern wave systems, and the speeds at which they reinforce (humps) or cancel (hollows) behind the ship.

6. Wave energy

Potential and kinetic energy of a wave, E = ½ρga², the energy flux, and why wave energy travels at the group velocity.
