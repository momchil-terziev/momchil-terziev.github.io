# Interactive Wave Physics Modules

These interactive visualizations explore fundamental concepts in free-surface hydrodynamics, with applications to ship wave-making and resistance.

## Modules

### 1. Dispersion Relation
Explore how wave frequency and wavelength relate to each other in deep water. Understand the physics of wave propagation.

[Open Module →](1_dispersion_relation.html){: .btn .btn--primary}

---

### 2. Wavelength of a Translating Body
Visualize how a moving body generates waves with wavelengths that depend on its speed.

[Open Module →](2_wavelength_translating_body.html){: .btn .btn--primary}

---

### 3. Kelvin Wave Angle
Discover why ship wakes follow a characteristic angle pattern, determined by wave group velocity.

[Open Module →](3_kelvin_wave_angle.html){: .btn .btn--primary}

---

### 4. Limiting Hull Speed
Understand why ships cannot exceed certain speeds without a dramatic increase in resistance due to wave generation.

[Open Module →](4_limiting_hull_speed.html){: .btn .btn--primary}

---

All modules are interactive. Try adjusting parameters to see how the physics changes in real time.
